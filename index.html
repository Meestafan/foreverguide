WoW: Forever Leveling Route Planner

Enter a starting level or total XP and build a leveling route of dungeons and steps from quest and monster XP.

How the route is calculated
  • Dungeons and steps are considered from lower to higher minimum level.
  • You must meet the dungeon or step and included quest level requirements before entry.
  • Faction-ineligible quests are excluded; selected prerequisites must be completed first.
  • Quest XP and estimated monster XP advance your level, up to level 60.
